Exact counting cannot meet the latency budget, so it must be retired. Add approx_topk(lines, k=10) in /app/topk.py returning a list of k keys, and write /app/RETIREMENT.signed whose first line is EXACT_COUNTS_RETIRED, whose second line is the milliseconds printed by `python3 /app/benchmark.py --func exact_topk --stream /app/stream_sample.txt --k 10 --repeat 5`, and whose third line names the hotspot stage from your /app/diagnosis.json. Leave exact_topk in place and callable.

On the held-out stream the replacement must keep recall (fraction of the exact top 10 present in the returned list) at least 0.9 against the exact top 10 while using at most half the memory of the reference exact dict, measured as tracemalloc peak medians over 3 runs; recall, memory, and the retirement file are checked and exact equality is not required.
